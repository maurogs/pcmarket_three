# Buyer protection

_This text is a template generated by the platform and is pending review by legal counsel before its definitive publication._

## What buyer protection is and when it applies

Buyer protection is an internal mechanism of {{site_name}} for recovering your money when an order **paid through the platform** (checkout with integrated payment) goes wrong. Its purpose is to give confidence to transactions that take place within the platform.

**Important:** it only protects orders paid through the platform. If the payment was made directly between users (bank transfer, cash on collection or any other means agreed outside {{site_name}}), this protection does not apply: the relationship falls outside the platform's reach.

## What it covers

You may rely on buyer protection when:

- **You do not receive the product:** the order paid through the platform does not arrive or is not delivered.
- **The product does not correspond significantly to the listing:** different category or model, advertised parts or accessories are missing, condition very different from the one described (for example, advertised as "like new" but it arrives with serious unmentioned damage).
- **The product arrives damaged in transit:** damage attributable to the shipment, not to subsequent use.

It is not a guarantee against professional sellers (who remain liable under consumer legislation): it is the fast, free route for incidents within the platform.

## Inspection window: 48 hours after delivery

On receiving the order, inspect it as soon as possible. You may report a problem **within the 48 hours following delivery**: after that period passes without an incident, the order is treated as completed. Inspect the parcel the same day: open the box, check the model and the condition, and take photographs before assembling or using anything if you spot a problem.

## How to open a claim

1. Go to **My orders** and open the affected order.
2. Press the incident option ("report a problem"), stating what happened: not delivered, not as described, or damaged.
3. Provide evidence: photographs of the product and of the packaging, the specific reason and, if the listing described something else, a screenshot or reference to the original description.
4. The seller may respond with a proposal.
5. If you do not reach an agreement, the {{site_name}} team reviews the case with the information from both parties (human moderation) and resolves it. If the claim succeeds and the payment was made through the platform, the refund is managed through the same payment method used, via the payment provider.

Decisions are given with reasons, and you may appeal them through the complaints route in the terms of use.

## Exclusions

Buyer protection does not cover, among other situations:

- orders paid directly between the parties, outside the platform's checkout;
- defects or faults that the listing expressly described ("as described" informed purchases);
- damage caused by the buyer after delivery, or improper use of the product;
- wear consistent with the condition (second-hand) advertised;
- claims submitted after the 48-hour window has closed;
- differences in colour shade or minor details that do not significantly affect the correspondence with the listing.

## Direct payment mode: safety advice

When the pay-through-the-platform feature is not available, or the parties prefer to pay each other directly, bear in mind that buyer protection does not apply. Recommendations:

- inspect the product in person before paying whenever you can (cash on collection);
- meet in a public, safe place for in-person exchanges;
- do not make advance transfers to strangers, and do not accept advance payments outside the platform;
- be wary of prices clearly off the market, or of anyone insisting on leaving {{site_name}} to negotiate;
- use {{site_name}}'s channels to communicate: internal messages help prove what was agreed.
