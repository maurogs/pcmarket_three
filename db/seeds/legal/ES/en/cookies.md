## What are cookies?

Cookies are small files that a website stores on your device (computer, tablet or phone) when you visit it. They serve to remember information about your visit, such as your language, your cart or your preferences, and to make the site work securely. On {{site_name}} we only use our own cookies (issued by us, never by third parties) and, for the time being, exclusively necessary technical cookies.
