## Two different situations: read this before requesting a return

On {{site_name}} the products are sold by other users, and the applicable rules change depending on who is selling:

- **Sale between private individuals (C2C):** the seller is a private individual who is not acting as a business or professional. Between private individuals there is no statutory right of cancellation.
- **Sale by a professional seller (B2C):** the seller is a business or professional (identified as such on the platform). Consumer legislation applies, including the right of cancellation and the statutory guarantee.

The identity of the seller and the type of sale appear in the listing and in the order details.

## Purchases from private sellers

Since there is no statutory right of cancellation between consumers, a return depends on the agreement with the seller. However, if the order was paid through the platform, our buyer protection policy applies (published in the section "Buyer protection"): you can report in the app that the product did not arrive, arrived damaged or does not correspond significantly to the listing, within the period stated in that policy. We recommend that you inspect the product as soon as you receive it.

If payment was made directly between the parties (outside the platform), we suggest you contact the other party through {{site_name}}'s messaging and, failing agreement, consult the ordinary legal routes.

## Purchases from professional sellers: right of cancellation

Under Articles 102 to 108 of Royal Legislative Decree 1/2007 (the "TRLGDCU", the Spanish consumer protection act), you have **14 calendar days** from receipt of the goods to cancel the purchase without giving a reason. To exercise the right:

- notify us unambiguously (through the order's internal messaging, or by writing to {{contact_email}}), if you prefer using the model cancellation form set out in the TRLGDCU itself;
- return the product within a maximum of 14 days from exercising the cancellation, in conditions normal for resale; the direct cost of the return is borne by the consumer, unless the seller applies a different criterion;
- the professional seller must refund the price paid within a maximum of 14 days, without undue charges.

There are statutory exceptions (Article 103 TRLGDCU), such as personalised products, and recordings and software that you have unsealed.

## Statutory conformity guarantee with professional sellers

All products sold by professionals benefit from the statutory conformity guarantee regulated in Article 120 of the TRLGDCU (as worded by Royal Decree-Law 7/2021):

- **New products:** 3 years' guarantee from delivery.
- **Second-hand products:** the parties may agree a reduced period, which may never be less than 1 year.

If the product is not in conformity (it is not fit for its ordinary use, it does not have the advertised features, etc.), you may require its repair or replacement; if that is not possible or is not resolved properly, a price reduction or termination of the contract (return and refund). Defects appearing within the statutory periods are presumed to have pre-existed. A private seller does not give this statutory guarantee: as against them, buyer protection and the general rules of the Civil Code (the Spanish Civil Code) apply.

## How to request a return or a cancellation in the app

1. Go to **My orders** and open the affected order.
2. Use the order's reporting or incident option, stating the reason; in purchases from professionals, state that you are exercising the right of cancellation within the 14 days.
3. Attach photographs or evidence where they help explain the problem.
4. The other party may respond; if you do not reach an agreement and the order was paid through the platform, the {{site_name}} team will review the case and resolve it under the buyer protection policy.

Keep the packaging and the proof of delivery while an incident remains open.
