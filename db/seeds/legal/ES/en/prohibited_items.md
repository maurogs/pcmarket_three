# Prohibited items

_This text is a template generated by the platform and is pending review by legal counsel before its definitive publication._

This policy lists the products that **may not be published or sold** on {{site_name}}. Listings are reviewed before publication, and removed as soon as we detect an infringement or receive a well-founded report. Publishing prohibited items may lead to removal of the listing, suspension or closure of the account and, where appropriate, referral to the authorities.

## Stolen goods, illicit provenance or no title to own

- Stolen or lost products, and any equipment whose illicit origin is reasonably knowable.
- Items you do not own and have no right to dispose of: other people's consignment goods without authorisation, equipment under retention of title or sealed by financial entities, and goods delivered by deposit, loan for use or consignment without their owner's authorisation.
- Equipment with activation locks or linked to third-party accounts (for example, locks for unpaid instalments) that prevent its legitimate use.

## Counterfeits, replicas and circumvention of protections

- Counterfeit products or replicas that imitate third-party brands, including accessories and packaging.
- Consoles, phones or any device modified (chips, hardware or software modifications) to circumvent their protection measures, run unauthorised software or connect to online services contrary to their terms.
- Pirated software, licences or product keys obtained or generated illicitly, unauthorised distribution accounts, and guides or methods for activating products in an unauthorised way.

## Accounts and video game assets

- User accounts for platforms, stores or games (sale, purchase or rental of accounts).
- In-game currency, items or other virtual assets redeemable within video games ("gold", paid boosting involving transfer of assets), where their transmission contravenes the service terms or fosters fraudulent practices.

## Weapons, dangerous material and security

- Firearms, licensed air weapons, ammunition, explosives and their components.
- Items that may be classified as a weapon (for example, batons or specifically offensive equipment).

## Recalled, unsafe or non-compliant products

- Products subject to a recall or safety alert (for example, batteries or chargers included on EU dangerous product lists).
- Swollen, damaged or modified lithium batteries: they present a fire risk and must not be advertised.
- Electrical equipment that does not carry the CE marking or meet other applicable safety requirements.

## Other restricted items

- Sexual or adult content.
- Items whose possession or sale requires a licence, authorisation or special approval, without it being evidenced.
- Personal data, identity documents and any information that infringes privacy (for example, third-party databases).
- Gift cards, vouchers or prepaid cards with balance: not accepted on the platform because of their high fraud risk.
- Any product whose offer or sale is contrary to Spanish or European Union legislation.

## How to report a prohibited listing

If you see a listing that breaches this list, use the **"Report"** button on the listing itself, or write to {{contact_email}} stating the address of the listing and the facts that make it unlawful or contrary to these rules (it is a "notice" under Article 16 of Regulation (EU) 2022/2065, the Digital Services Act). We will review the communication diligently, act within a reasonable period and, if we remove content following a report, inform the affected person. Repeated unfounded notices may lead to measures against the reporting account (Article 23 of the DSA).
