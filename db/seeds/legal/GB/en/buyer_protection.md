# Buyer protection

## What buyer protection is and when it applies

Buyer protection is {{site_name}}'s internal mechanism for getting your money back when an order **paid through the platform** (integrated checkout) goes wrong. Its purpose is to make transactions that stay inside the platform safer.

**Important:** it only covers orders paid through the platform. If you paid the other user directly (bank transfer, cash on collection or anything else agreed outside {{site_name}}), buyer protection does not apply: the transaction is outside our reach.

## What it covers

Buyer protection is available when:

- **You never receive the item:** an order paid through the platform does not arrive or is not delivered.
- **The item is significantly not as described:** different category or model, advertised parts or accessories missing, condition very different from the listing (for example, listed "like new" but it arrives with serious damage that was not mentioned).
- **The item arrives damaged in transit:** damage caused by the delivery, not by later use.

It is not a substitute for traders' obligations (traders remain liable under the Consumer Rights Act 2015 and the Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013): it is the fast, free route for problems inside the platform.

## Inspection window: 48 hours after delivery

Check your order as soon as it lands. You can report a problem **within 48 hours of delivery**: once that window closes with no report, the order is treated as complete. Open the parcel the same day, check the model and condition, and photograph everything before fitting or using anything if you spot a problem.

## How to raise a claim

1. Go to **My orders** and open the order concerned.
2. Tap the incident option ("report a problem") and say what happened: not delivered, not as described, or damaged.
3. Provide evidence: photos of the item and the packaging, the specific problem and, if the listing said something different, a screenshot or reference to the original description.
4. The seller may respond with a proposal.
5. If you cannot agree, the {{site_name}} team reviews the case using both sides' information (human moderation) and decides. If the claim succeeds and the payment went through the platform, the refund is processed through the payment provider, back to the original payment method.

Decisions come with reasons, and you can appeal them through the complaints route in the Terms of use (including our Online Safety Act 2023 complaints and appeals procedure).

## Exclusions

Buyer protection does not cover, among other cases:

- orders paid directly between the parties, outside the platform's checkout;
- faults or defects the listing expressly described (informed "sold as seen" purchases);
- damage you cause after delivery, or misuse of the product;
- wear consistent with the advertised condition (second-hand);
- claims raised after the 48-hour window has closed;
- slight colour shade differences or minor details that do not make a material difference to the item matching the listing.

## Paying directly: safety tips

When pay-through-the-platform is unavailable, or you both prefer to settle directly, remember buyer protection will not apply. Our advice:

- inspect the item in person before paying where you can (collection in person);
- meet in a public, safe place for handovers;
- never send an advance bank transfer to someone you have never met, and do not take advance payments off-platform;
- be suspicious of prices well off the market, or of anyone pushing the deal off {{site_name}};
- keep chatting through {{site_name}}: in-app messages are the best evidence of what was agreed.
