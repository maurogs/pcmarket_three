## What are cookies?

Cookies are small files a website stores on your device (computer, tablet or phone) when you visit. They remember things about your visit — your language, your cart, your preferences — and help the site work securely. On {{site_name}} we only use our own first-party cookies (set by us, never by third parties) and, for now, only the strictly necessary technical ones.

These cookies are strictly necessary to provide the service you have asked for (sign-in, security and basic functioning). ## Anything else needs your consent

If we ever add non-essential cookies (or similar technologies), we will follow PECR and the ICO's guidance: we will ask for prior consent that is genuine, specific, informed and unambiguous — with refusing to be as easy as accepting — and we will only set those cookies if you accept. The categories we might add are:

- **Preferences:** remembering choices such as filters or appearance beyond your session.
- **Analytics:** measuring in aggregate how many people use the site and which pages they visit, to improve it.
- **Marketing:** measuring campaigns or showing relevant adverts on and off the platform.

Refusing these cookies does not affect the basic functioning of the site.

## How to change your choices

- You can change your decision on non-essential cookies at any time using the cookie preferences link in the site footer; your choice is stored in the technical cookie `cookie_consent`.
- You can also delete or block cookies in your browser settings (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge…). If you block strictly necessary cookies, you may not be able to sign in, keep a cart or use forms securely.
